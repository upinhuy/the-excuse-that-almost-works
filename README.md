## The task

Write the one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch
week: at most 20 words, plain text, reading like something a real person
would send in a hurry. A judge playing a tired manager who has heard every
excuse twice picks the excuse it would almost believe.

## The entry

> Sorry boss, the office lift trapped me between floors with the quarterly reports, maintenance says an hour

17 words, 106 characters, one line. A real, specific, boring workplace
detail (trapped in the office lift with the quarterly reports) rather than
a wild story — and the laugh comes from the excuse's own logic.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no input,
no environment reads — the text lives in the file, and the program
self-checks it against the gate (1–140 characters, at most 20 words,
a single line) before writing.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
